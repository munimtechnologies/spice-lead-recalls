Import Alert # 28-13
Published Date: 07/09/2021
Type: DWPE
Import Alert Name:
"DETENTION WITHOUT PHYSICAL EXAMINATION OF SPICES AND SPICE PRODUCTS DUE TO LEAD CONTAMINATION"
Reason for Alert:
NOTE: The revision of this Import Alert, dated 06/22/2017, updates the Alert from being specific for turmeric to include other spices and spice products. Changes are bracketed by asterisks (***).
The revision of this Import Alert, dated 08/10/2020, updates the name of the alert, reason for alert, guidance, contact information, and the charge section. Changes are bracketed by asterisks (***).
As part of its responsibilities for ensuring the safety of the U.S. food supply, the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) monitors levels of certain toxic elements in foods. FDA uses this information to estimate dietary exposure to these contaminants and to identify the relative contributions of these foods to total toxic element exposure. With regard to lead, particular emphasis is placed on foods consumed by children, who are the most sensitive to its adverse health effects.
In 2013, the FDA was notified via the Reportable Food Registry/Risk Control Review of a specific instance of a child found with elevated levels of lead after consuming turmeric spice imported from Bangladesh. Subsequent FDA sampling of imported turmeric products revealed various products having levels of lead that may render the products injurious to health.
In 2017, sampling by FDA and State Departments of Health revealed additional types of spices and spice products having high levels of lead that may render the products injurious to health.
Based on the findings in the additional spice products, this import alert has been modified to cover specific firms that have offered spices and spice products with levels of lead that may render a product injurious to health for import into the United States and have met the criteria for detention without physical examination as established in FDA's Regulatory Procedures Manual (RPM) Chapter ***9-8***.
In making a determination as to whether a level of lead in a spice product may render that product injurious to health, FDA takes into account the amount of the metal present in the product, consumption data available for that product, and the segment of the population to be most at risk if they consume the product.
Note: ORS contact information updated on 08/16/2019
Guidance:
Divisions may detain without physical examination shipments of identified products from the firms identified on the Red List of this Alert.
Recommendations for additions to the Red List should be forwarded to the Division of Import Operations (DIO). DIO and FDA's Center for Food and Safety and Applied Nutrition (CFSAN) will evaluate regulatory packages on a case-by-case basis.
Release of Articles Subject to Detention without Physical Examination under this Import Alert:
In order to secure release of an individual shipment subject to detention without physical examination under this alert, the owner, consignee and/or another responsible party to the shipment should provide the results of private laboratory analysis of a representative sample of the shipment, which demonstrates that the product does not contain a level of lead that may render it injurious to health.***Resources for private laboratory testing are available at:
https://www.fda.gov/science-research/field-science-and-laboratories/private-laboratory-testing ***
Removal from Detention without Physical Examination (REMOVE FROM RED LIST):
In order to remove a firm/product from detention without physical examination, information should be provided to FDA to adequately demonstrate that the firm in question has resolved the conditions that gave rise to the appearance of the violation, so that the Agency will have confidence that future entries will be in compliance. For further guidance on removal from detention without physical examination, refer to FDAs Regulatory Procedures Manual (RPM), Chapter 9-8, "Detention without Physical Examination (DWPE)."
If a firm and/or a representative thereof would like to request removal from detention without physical examination under this Import Alert, all relevant information supporting the request should be forward to the following address:
