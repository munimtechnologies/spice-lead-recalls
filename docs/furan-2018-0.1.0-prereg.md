What was seen of the file before writing this, stated plainly: a text preview of its structure
(header, the first eight rows, which product label each run of sample numbers carries, and two
row-level oddities: sample C2018FRAN00172 has two 3-methylfuran rows and no 2-methylfuran row, and
C2018FRAN00170's furan row has an empty date and test type). The first eight rows show the values
of samples 1 and 2; their sums (62.13 and 27.833) appear in the report's Figure 1. No total,
count of detections, mean, minimum or maximum was computed from the file.

The grading code is `furan_2018.py`, and its offline tests are `test_furan_2018.py`. A sealed
compute job (`furan-2018-v1`) grades this registration only after it is public on GitHub. The job
checks out the published code, then downloads the file and reads it.

Nothing here is a finding about any product, brand or health risk. Health Canada judged the
levels in this survey not a health concern. Canada has no maximum levels for furan or the
methylfurans.

## Why this is new

Searches on 2026-10-30 found the CFIA report page, the open dataset, the 2023-24 follow-up
report, EFSA's 2017 opinion and reviews of furan occurrence. They found no analysis of this file,

## Already visible from the printed figures alone

1. **The detected column adds to 175, not 176.** Table 2's rows give 50 + 50 + 10 + 15 + 0 + 50
   = 175 detected samples, and Figure 1 lists 175 values, but the grand total and the text say 176.
2. **The dried-beverage average is the 15 printed values divided by 16.** Figure 1's 15
   beverage values sum to 155.18 ppb: their average is 10.35, not the printed 9.7, while
   155.18 / 16 = 9.70. The other four rows' Figure 1 averages reproduce Table 2 (46.60, 31.14,
   19.85, 73.82).
3. **The grand-total average does not fit.** Figure 1's 175 values average 45.32 ppb (45.07 over
   176); the per-row averages imply 45.0 to 45.3. The printed figure is 45.5.
4. **Table 3 counts 43 dried beverage samples; Tables 1 and 2 count 42.**
5. **The percentages cannot be shares of all 250 samples.** If 52% of 250 (130) had a single
   analogue, at most 46 of the 176 detected samples could hold two or more, fewer than the 95 that
   38% of 250 would need. Read as shares of the detected samples, they fit (about 92 and 67).
6. Figure 1's canned-meat list prints "38" after "38.17", out of its otherwise rising order.

## Rules (fixed now)

- **Columns** by header: `Sample No.`, `Product` (English), `Component` (English),
  `MaxOfAMOUNT`, `Report Unit`, `PLAN_CODE`; also `Origin`, `Function`, `Date Sampled`.
- **Analytes:** component `Furan`, `2-Methylfuran` or `3-Methylfuran`. Each analyte's value is the
  largest of its rows; a missing analyte is left out of the sum (not set to 0).
- **Value rule:** a number above 0 is detected; 0, a `<` with a number >= 0 or a not-detected
  token is not detected; anything else fails the gate.
- **Furans** = the sum of a sample's analyte values; a sample is detected if its furans level is
  above 0. Units: ng/g = ppb.
- **Product types:** `Fish/Seafood - Canned` = canned fish; `Meat - Canned` = canned meat;
  `Sauce - Soy` = soy sauce; `Evaporated Milk` = canned milk; `Milk Powder` = dried dairy
  products; `Beverage - Powdered Mixes - Hot` and `- Cold` = dried beverage mix. **`Dairy
  Alternative` is split by rule:** a sample with furans detected counts as canned milk, one
  without as a dried dairy product (Table 2 prints 0 of 42 dried dairy products with furans).
  Labels are compared after trimming spaces and reading en or em dashes as hyphens.
- **"Matches"** means the value rounds to the printed figure at its printed precision (for
  example 5.05-5.15 for "5.1", 63.5-64.5 for "64").
- **Figure 1 matching:** each printed value is paired with a distinct detected sample of its
  product type that matches it, most precise printed values first, closest value winning.

## Gate (G)

File size 133,082 bytes (the portal's record; the MD5 is compared and reported, not gated);
the five key columns found (sample, product, component, value, unit); 750 rows; samples exactly C2018FRAN00001 to C2018FRAN00250; only the
three analyte labels; unit `ng/g` and plan `2018_TS023` on every row; only the eight known
product labels; every value passing the value rule. If G fails, nothing else is graded.

## Predictions (P1-P4)

- **P1.** The file has **176** samples with a furans level above 0, the printed total and not the
  175 its rows add to. The extra one is a dried beverage mix at **0.82 ppb or less**, absent from
  Figure 1 and from the "15 detected" count, which the printed 9.7 ppb average includes (16
  detected beverage mixes whose average matches 9.7).
- **P2.** The average over all detected samples is **45.0 to 45.4 ppb**, not the printed 45.5.
- **P3.** The text's 52% (one analogue only) and 38% (furan and 2-methylfuran both detected)
  match as shares of the detected samples and do not match as shares of all 250.
- **P4.** Labels: **15** Evaporated Milk, **38** Milk Powder, **5** Dairy Alternative and **42**
  powdered beverage mixes (so Table 3's 43 is a slip). **Exactly one** Dairy Alternative has
  furans detected, which makes canned milk 16 and dried dairy products 42.

Competing reading for P1, stated now: if the file has 175 detected samples, the 176 and the 9.7
are slips on the page (a count and an average divided by 16 instead of 15), and P1 fails.
