This completes the PAH 2019-20 part of the [bpa-pah-report 0.1.0 pre-registration](bpa-pah-report-0.1.0-prereg.md)
(`v0.4.86`, predictions D2-D5). The [census note](bpa-pah-report-0.1.0-census.md) graded D1 (296
samples, 34 / 114 / 148). The label map and the [grader note](pah-2019-0.1.0-grader.md) (`v0.4.94`)
were public before any value was read. The first sealed run failed its gate only because two
sample IDs end in `AD`. The [ID-pattern amendment](id-pattern-0.1.1-amendment.md) (`v0.4.97`)
widened that one pattern, and the published grader ran unchanged.

The sealed job `pah-2019-regrade-v1` ran on 2026-11-01 from 14:22 to 14:23 UTC (python:3.12,
1 GB, no host paths). It waited until the four notes were public and checked its modules against
the published copies. It ran the 10 grader tests and the 8 amendment tests (all passed), and only
then read the file (MD5 `0bb033bbcdab8305839d0a8716def1b3`, the same as the portal record).

**The gate passes, and all 3 graded predictions held (D2, D3, D4).** Table 2 reproduces in all 20
of its cells. The report's text sentences about formula, nuts and olive oil all hold.

This is a reproducibility check of open government data. It is not a compliance finding, it is
not about any brand, and it is not dietary or medical advice. All values are ng/g (ppb). "TEQ" is
benzo[a]pyrene + 0.1 x benz[a]anthracene + 0.1 x benzo[b]fluoranthene + 0.01 x chrysene. A sample
counts as positive when its TEQ is above zero. Averages are over positive samples only.

## Headlines

1. **Table 2 reproduces in full.** Infant formula: 34 samples, 16 positive, TEQ 0.001-0.776,
   average 0.12. Nuts, seeds and butters: 114, 44, 0.001-2.93, average 0.17. Olive oil: 148, 121,
   0.002-3.25, average 0.24. All samples: 296, 181, 0.001-3.25, average 0.21. All 20 cells
   (n, positives, minimum, maximum, average) match within the printed rounding.
2. **Table 3's olive-oil average of 0.23 is a truncation.** The file's olive-oil average is
   0.2373. Rounded, that is Table 2's 0.24. Cut off after two decimals, it is Table 3's 0.23. The
   pre-registration made no prediction on which table the file supports.
3. **The text's specific claims hold.** 16 of 23 milk-based formulas are positive (maximum 0.776)
   and no soy formula is. The highest nut product is a Canadian pumpkin seed butter (TEQ 2.93),
   and the next is a tahini at 0.7728, under the 0.773 the registration allowed. Exactly three
   olive oils have a TEQ of 0.700 or more (3.25, 2.32 and 2.22); the fourth is 0.6996.
4. **A third pair of different samples with identical results.** Two imported olive oils,
   `C2019PAHS00123A` (origin unknown, sampled 15 April 2019) and `C2019PAHS00147A` (Argentina,
   7 April 2019), have identical values for all 16 PAHs. Seven of the values are above zero, each
   given to four decimals (for example naphthalene 16.4111, phenanthrene 13.4442). No other two
   samples with any PAH above zero share all 16 values. The 2018-19 PAH file had two such pairs
   ([twin scan](pah-2018-0.1.0-twin-scan.md)). Identical values at this precision in two samples
   of different origin point to a copied row, but the file cannot say which sample is right.
5. **The two `AD` samples have no `A` partner.** `C2019PAHS00037AD` (Tunisia) and
   `C2019PAHS00040AD` (Morocco) are imported olive oils, both sampled on 4 September 2019, and
   neither has any of the four TEQ PAHs above zero. Neither `00037A` nor `00040A` is in the file,
   so the suffix does not mark a duplicate analysis of a sample that is also listed. The 296
   samples use the numbers 1 to 300; 53, 68, 194 and 264 are missing.
6. **Table 1's origin split is off by one and two samples.** Using the file's Function column,
   infant formula is 0 domestic / 32 imported / 2 unknown (Table 1: 0 / 31 / 3) and olive oil is
   1 / 135 / 12 (Table 1: 1 / 133 / 14). Nuts match exactly (40 / 46 / 28). This was reported in
   the grader note as ungraded, and every total matches.
7. **The dates look like real sampling dates.** They run from 7 April 2019 to 11 March 2020,
   inside the 2019-20 fiscal year. There are 132 different dates, with up to 9 samples on one day.
   Unlike the 2009-10 bisphenol A file ([regrade result](bpa-series-0.1.1-regrade-result.md)), they
   do not follow the sample-ID order.

**D5 (descriptive).** The report compares olive oils with the EU maximum levels for oils and fats:
benzo[a]pyrene 2.0 ng/g and the sum of four PAHs (PAH4) 10.0 ng/g. Canada has no such limit for
olive oil, so this is only a description. One olive oil has benzo[a]pyrene above 2.0: an imported
Greek oil at 3.1 (`C2019PAHS00056A`). A Turkish oil (`00048A`) is at exactly 2.0. Two have PAH4
above 10.0: an import of unknown origin at 12.05 (`00104A`) and a Lebanese oil at 10.43 (`00115A`).
The Lebanese oil has no benzo[a]pyrene; its PAH4 comes mostly from chrysene (7.68).

Benzo[a]pyrene alone is above zero in 32 of 148 olive oils (maximum 3.1), 8 of 114 nut products
(maximum 2.45) and 4 of 34 formulas (maximum 0.44).

## Limits

- "Positive" is the file's own coding (a value above zero). The file gives no reporting limit.
- The pomace clause in D4 (four pomace oils, average 0.19, maximum 0.569) cannot be graded: no
  product label separates pomace oil from other olive oil.
- Headlines 2 and 4-7 were checked after grading. Only the twin scan, the dates and the origin
  split were named in advance as descriptive checks.
- The truncation reading of Table 3 fits the one figure available. It is not proven.

## Reproduce it

- `data/pah_2019_samples.csv` is the sealed job's per-sample table: ID, product, group, formula
  type, Function, origin, date, TEQ, PAH4, the number of PAHs above zero, and all 16 PAH values in
  ppb.
- `data/pah_2019_summary.json` is the grader's full record. `data/pah_2019_id_amendment.json` is
  the amendment's record of the two `AD` IDs.
- `test_pah_2019_result.py` does not import the grader. From the per-sample table it recomputes
  each sample's TEQ and PAH4 from the 16 PAH columns. It then rebuilds Table 2, the text checks,
  D5, the twin pair, the ID numbers and the dates with its own code, and checks them against the
  sealed record.

A sealed follow-up job (`pah-2019-result-v1`) fetches the published files from GitHub and runs
this test.
